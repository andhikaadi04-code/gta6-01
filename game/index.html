SOLMAR BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

You are Marco Reyes, a wheelman new in town. Lena Voss has work for you: wheels to steal, packages to run, and the Solmar Bay police to outrun. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel — and mind your wanted level.

WASD — walk / drive  ·  Mouse — look around (click the game to lock the mouse)
E — steal / exit a car  ·  Shift — run  ·  Space — jump / handbrake
F — punch  ·  Left click — shoot  ·  R — reload
P — pause  ·  M — mute  ·  Your progress saves automatically.
